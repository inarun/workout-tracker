Ledger support

Ledger (Lifting Ledger on the App Store) is a workout log for iPhone and Apple Watch. It is made and run by one person, so support is by email.

Contact

Email support@nusayb.me. Say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened, and add a screenshot if you can. Your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (Settings › About in Ledger) help too.

If you're testing a beta, you can also send feedback from TestFlight: take a screenshot, then choose Share Beta Feedback.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Ledger works fully offline without one. An account is optional and only keeps your own log in sync across your devices.
  • How do I back up or move my log? In Settings, use Export Backup for a full backup file or Export CSV for a spreadsheet. Import a backup from Settings on another device.
  • How do I delete my account and data? In Settings › Account, choose Delete Account. It deletes every cloud record and then the account. You can also ask by email from the address on the account.
  • The Apple Watch app shows nothing. The watch app needs watchOS 27 and your iPhone nearby. Start a workout on the iPhone or the watch and it appears on both.
  • Ask Ledger is missing. The Apple Intelligence features need iOS 27, a supported iPhone, and Apple Intelligence turned on in the Settings app.
  • Apple Health. Health is off until you turn it on in Settings › Apple Health in Ledger. You can change what Ledger may read and write in the Health app at any time.
